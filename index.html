What to Expect When You Return

Even if the fire seemed small, your home may look very different now. Firefighters often have to break windows or cut holes in the roof to release dangerous heat and smoke. We also check for hidden fire inside walls, which may require removing sheetrock.

Please be careful: Soot and dirty water left behind can contain materials that may make you sick. It is best to minimize your time inside until it has been cleaned.

🛑 SAFETY FIRST

Do not enter your damaged home unless the Fire Department explicitly says it is safe.

  • Roofs and floors may be weakened.
  • Do not clean up or repair anything until the fire investigation is officially completed.
  • You may temporarily cover broken windows or holes to prevent weather damage, but do not disturb the scene.

Immediate Next Steps

🐾 Care for Your Pets

Scared animals often react by biting or scratching. Handle them gently.

Tip: Contact your vet to assess their overall health if they were exposed to smoke. Try to leave them with family or friends while you handle immediate logistics.

🔒 Secure Your Property

If you must leave your home vacant, notify the police to prevent looting. You may need to board up windows/doors to secure the premises.

Call Denton PD (Non-Emergency) →

📞 Contact Your Insurance

Notify them of the fire immediately. Ask specifically about "Loss of Use" coverage—this can often provide immediate funds for hotels, food, and clothes.

Note: Ask for a cost estimate in writing from any restoration company before they begin work.

Rebuilding & Permits

The City of Denton requires a building permit to repair fire or other types of damage. We are happy to help with the list of repairs needed along with any code updates to your home. Code updates, if required, will be minimal and will make your home safer — some of these updates may include installing smoke detectors, carbon monoxide detectors, and updated electrical wiring.

Please know that we are here to help.

Disaster / Fire Damage — City of Denton →

First Steps

  1. Call Building Safety to request a courtesy fire/disaster inspection. A staff member will help you schedule it.
    📞 (940) 349-8600
  2. Full loss? You will need a permit to demolish the damaged structure and then a separate permit to rebuild when you are ready.
    Demolition Permit → | New Construction Permit →
  3. After the courtesy inspection, a list of necessary repairs will be provided to you. Share this list with your contractor and insurance adjuster.
  4. Your selected contractor will submit a building permit application with a floor plan and repair details. You or your contractor can meet with a plans examiner if you need guidance on what to submit.
    Residential Additions or Alterations →
  5. All contractors must be registered with the City to ensure correct licenses are held.
    Contractor Registration →

During Construction

  • Ensure all required inspections are completed at each stage.
  • Submit all required inspection reports to your insurance adjuster.
View Inspections List (PDF) →

Completion

Once all work is done, the City inspector will issue a final inspection report. A copy must be sent to your insurance adjuster to close out your claim.
